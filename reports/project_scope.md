# CareerPulse AI - Project Scope

## Problem Statement
Students and early-career candidates often find it difficult to understand changing job-market requirements, identify important skills, and measure the gap between their current skills and target roles.

## Proposed Solution
CareerPulse AI combines data analytics, NLP and machine learning to transform job-market data into skill-demand insights, salary analytics, job categories, career matching and skill-gap reports.

## EDI / Entrepreneurship Angle
Potential users:
- Students
- Colleges and placement cells
- Training institutes
- Career guidance services
- Recruitment analytics teams

Potential product model:
- Student career dashboard
- College placement intelligence
- Training recommendation service
- Analytics subscription for institutions

## ML/NLP Components
- TF-IDF + Logistic Regression for job-category classification
- Random Forest regression for salary estimation
- Rule/NLP skill extraction
- Skill-match recommendation engine
